# Microcopy That Converts: Small Words, Big Difference

The tiny words around your buttons, forms, and error messages do more persuading than your hero headline ever will. "Submit" says nothing; "Get my free audit" tells the user exactly what happens next — and removes the fear of the unknown. In forms, replace bare labels with guidance: instead of "Email", try "Work email — we'll only send your report". Error messages should explain the fix, not the failure: "That password needs at least 8 characters" beats "Invalid input" every time. Good microcopy is written from the user's anxiety, not the system's logic: at every step, ask "what would make me hesitate here?" — then answer it in a few words, right where the hesitation happens.

**Why it matters:** Microcopy is the cheapest conversion optimization you have — a few changed words can lift completion rates without touching design or code.
