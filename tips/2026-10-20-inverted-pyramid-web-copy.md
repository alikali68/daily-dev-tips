# Front-load your copy: the inverted pyramid wins on the web

Most readers decide within seconds whether your copy deserves their time. The inverted pyramid — conclusion first, details after — respects that reality.

Lead with the outcome or key message. Follow with the supporting points that prove it. Push the background and nice-to-knows to the bottom. Every paragraph should survive the reader skipping everything below it — if the first sentence of each paragraph tells the whole story on its own, your structure works.

This also helps SEO: your key terms appear early, exactly where both search engines and skimmers look.

**Quick test:** read only the first sentence of each paragraph in your draft. If the story still lands, ship it. If not, rewrite from the top.

**Why it matters:** People scan, they don't read — copy that delivers value up front converts more readers and loses fewer of them.
