# Kerning and Tracking: The Invisible Polish in Typography

Kerning adjusts the space between individual letter pairs — "AV" or "To" often need tightening because their shapes leave awkward visual gaps. Tracking adjusts spacing evenly across a whole word or block of text, and it is what makes all-caps headlines readable instead of cramped. Use tracking to open up uppercase headings (+20 to +50 in most design tools), and kern by eye at large display sizes, where every gap is visible. The test is simple: squint at the text — if any single gap jumps out, it needs work. Good spacing is invisible; bad spacing is the first thing people feel, even if they cannot name it.

**Why it matters:** Typography carries the tone of a design before a single word is read — clean spacing is what separates professional work from amateur work at a glance.
