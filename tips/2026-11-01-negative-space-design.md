# Negative Space Is a Design Element, Not Empty Space

Beginners fill every pixel; professionals design the gaps. Negative space — the breathing room around and between elements — is what guides the eye, separates ideas, and makes the important thing feel important. When a layout feels crowded, the fix is rarely a smaller font or a new color; it is usually removing an element or doubling the margin around the hero. A practical rule: increase spacing until the layout almost feels too airy, then pull back one step — that "almost too much" point is usually where hierarchy clicks. Treat whitespace as a first-class element in your grid, with the same intent you give to type and color.

**Why it matters:** Space creates hierarchy for free — no new assets, no extra code, just clearer attention.
