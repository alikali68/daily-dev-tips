# Write Headlines That Carry the Whole Story

Most readers skim before they commit. They scan your headings, first lines, and bold text — deciding in seconds whether the rest is worth their time.

Write so that a skim-reader still gets the full argument: make every heading a mini-summary ("Automate backups weekly" beats "Backups"), put the key takeaway in the first line of each paragraph, and cut throat-clearing intros entirely. If someone reads only your headings and first lines, they should still walk away with 80% of the value.

**Why it matters:** You're writing for people with 200 tabs open — respecting their attention is what turns skimmers into readers, and readers into clients.
